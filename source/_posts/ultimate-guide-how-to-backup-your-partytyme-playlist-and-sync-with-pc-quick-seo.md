One great subscriber benefit of the [Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service is the ability to export a PDF karaoke song book directly from the Party Tyme website. The songbook is updated in real-time so as soon as new songs are available in the subscription they are available in the downloadable (and printable) book.

All subscribers to the Party Tyme Karaoke subscription service need to do is log into the [www.partytymeplayer.com](http://www.partytymeplayer.com) website using the same user name and password credentials used to log into the service in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/) karaoke software, and the tools to create a (somewhat) customized karaoke songbook are readily available.

5) The ‘**Export To PDF**‘ menu allows you to select the ‘**Song List Type**‘ — Domestic, Foreign, or All — the ‘**Domestic Language**‘ — English or Spanish — and the ‘**Order By**‘ — Order by Artist or By Title. After dialing in your options, click the **OK** button. After clicking on **OK** the songbook creation process will begin.
